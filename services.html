5. Comprehensive Services

End-to-end infrastructure capabilities

Four capability lines covering a project from bare land to a signed completion certificate.

SERVICE 01

Turnkey Construction Contracts

Complete execution responsibility from ground excavation, RCC structural framing, brickwork and plastering, up to high-end architectural finishing for residential complexes and commercial towers.

  • Ground excavation & sub-structure work
  • RCC structural framing & brickwork
  • Plastering and high-end architectural finishing
  • Residential complexes & commercial towers
SERVICE 02

Land Development & Civil Infrastructure

Master layout planning, land plotting, N.A. (Non-Agricultural) clearance processing, internal asphalt/concrete road construction, stormwater drainage systems and water pipeline networks.

  • Master layout planning & land plotting
  • N.A. (Non-Agricultural) clearance processing
  • Internal asphalt / concrete road construction
  • Stormwater drainage & water pipeline networks
SERVICE 03

Architectural Design & Engineering Planning

Modern 3D architectural modeling, Vastu-compliant space design, MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) engineering and structural stability certifications by certified consultants.

  • Modern 3D architectural modeling
  • Vastu-compliant space design
  • MEP — Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing engineering
  • Structural stability certification by certified consultants
SERVICE 04

Statutory Clearances & Regulatory Approvals

End-to-end liaisoning and assistance for sanctions, so approvals never become the reason a project stalls.

  • Sanctions from PCMC and PMRDA
  • Collectorate approvals
  • N.A. status processing
  • Building completion certificates (CC/OC)
6. Land Acquisition & Feasibility

Risk-free & legally sound project initiation

Before a single footing is cast, we make sure the land is clean, correctly zoned and worth building on.

01 — DUE DILIGENCE

Title Due Diligence & Legal Verification

Rigorous legal checks of land ownership histories, 7/12 extracts, property mutation records, and encumbrance certificates to guarantee clear and marketable titles.

  • Ownership history verification
  • 7/12 extracts & mutation records
  • Encumbrance certificates
02 — LOCATION

Strategic Location Scouting

Identifying high-growth potential land parcels across Maharashtra that are ideally suited for residential townships, commercial hubs, and industrial warehousing.

  • Residential townships
  • Commercial hubs
  • Industrial warehousing
03 — FEASIBILITY

Feasibility & Negotiation Support

Comprehensive land surveys, zoning verifications, environmental clearances, and seamless stakeholder negotiations to secure optimal property rights and smooth project takeoff.

  • Land surveys & zoning verification
  • Environmental clearances
  • Stakeholder negotiation support
