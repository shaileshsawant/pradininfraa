1. Company Overview

Redefining Infrastructure Standards

Pradin Infraa is a premier infrastructure development and construction enterprise headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra. Established with an uncompromising commitment to redefining regional construction standards, the company specializes in executing end-to-end turnkey projects across residential, commercial, and heavy industrial domains.

Powered by precision structural engineering, state-of-the-art machinery, and rigorous quality assurance protocols, Pradin Infraa delivers durable, sustainable, and aesthetically superior infrastructure solutions aligned with highest global standards.

We serve individual homeowners, landowners, builders, developers and businesses across Pune district, Tal-Haveli and the wider Maharashtra region.
